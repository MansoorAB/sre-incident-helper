We are experiencing high latency (>200ms) in our 5G Core network affecting user data services. Multiple users have reported slow internet speeds and connection drops in the eastern region. Monitoring shows increased packet loss.

VoLTE service degradation reported in multiple locations. Call setup success rate has dropped to 85%. Users are experiencing call drops and poor voice quality. Initial monitoring shows IMS registration delays.

DNS resolution failures observed in the core network. About 30% of service requests are failing with timeout errors. This is causing authentication failures and service access issues for end users.

Network congestion alert in the transport network. Multiple cell sites reporting backhaul utilization >90%. Users experiencing slow data speeds and video buffering issues during peak hours.
